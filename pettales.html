🐶 Max the Golden Retriever

Story

Max was rescued from the streets and is now a therapy dog, bringing joy and comfort to hospital patients and elderly care homes. His gentle nature and loving personality make him perfect for this role.

Diet

  • High-quality chicken & rice formula
  • Daily vitamin supplements
  • Fresh water always available

🐱 Luna the Cat

Story

Luna was adopted from a shelter and has become the most affectionate companion. She loves to cuddle and has a special talent for knowing when someone needs emotional support.

Diet

  • Premium dry kibble
  • Weekly tuna treats
  • Fresh water daily

🐰 Bella the Bunny

Story

Bella was born in a pet-friendly farm and loves hopping around in the garden. She's super friendly with kids and has become a favorite among family visitors.

Diet

  • Fresh vegetables (carrots, lettuce)
  • High-quality hay
  • Occasional fruit treats

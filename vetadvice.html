Tips for Dogs

  • Include lean protein and rice in diet
  • Ensure regular exercise and playtime
  • Avoid chocolate, grapes, and onions
  • Regular vet check-ups are essential
🐱

Tips for Cats

  • Feed high-protein food
  • Fresh water always available
  • Include wet food for hydration
  • Avoid dog food and dairy products
🐰

Tips for Rabbits

  • 80% of diet should be hay
  • Fresh vegetables daily
  • Avoid iceberg lettuce
  • Regular dental checks important
🐦

Tips for Birds

  • Feed a balanced diet
  • Provide fresh water
  • Keep cage clean
  • Regular check-ups
